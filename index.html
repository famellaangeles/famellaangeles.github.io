About me

I’m a Marketing & Creative professional with experience turning ideas into marketing content that supports brand awareness, audience engagement, and business growth. My expertise spans social media management, content conceptualization, graphic design, video editing, website management, SEO-friendly blog writing, and email marketing.

Beyond content creation, I develop email campaigns and promotional materials, manage digital platforms, and use AI-powered tools to streamline creative workflows and marketing automation. I also monitor marketing analytics to evaluate content performance and support data-informed marketing decisions.

With a background in Business Administration majoring in Marketing and Advertising, I combine creativity with strategic thinking and adaptability. From developing campaign concepts to producing visual content and executing email marketing initiatives, I bring a hands-on approach to every project. My goal is to create meaningful marketing experiences that strengthen brands, connect with audiences, and contribute to measurable business results.

How I work

  • Full-spectrum marketer. I work across content creation, social media, email campaigns, website management, SEO, and digital marketing, connecting different disciplines to support a brand's wider goals.
  • Concept to execution. I turn marketing objectives into creative concepts, campaign messaging, visuals, videos, and promotional materials designed for their intended audience and platform.
  • Creative meets systems. I work with marketing platforms, AI tools, and automation to support smoother workflows, consistent communication, and more efficient campaign execution.
